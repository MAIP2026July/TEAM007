## Why this project?

In automotive software development, a change record can return from review even when the implementation work is largely finished, because important evidence is absent, not linked or written differently across teams. The idea is to flag likely gaps *before* the record reaches formal review. The product combines ordinary rules for well-defined checks with a **proposed** retrieval-grounded AI assessment for complex textual evidence, while retaining human accountability for compliance decisions.

## Try the prototype

Open [`prototype/index.html`](prototype/index.html) locally. Choose a complete, missing, ambiguous or unsupported synthetic case; assess it; record a human disposition; export the review summary as JSON. **This is a working rules-based interaction demonstrator, not a working AI compliance classifier.** It uses no real records or approved ASPICE criteria.

## Recommendation and limits

The next step is to validate a version-controlled criterion set, create an expert-labeled benchmark, compare the proposed AI-assisted workflow against manual and rules-only review, and complete confidentiality and governance approvals. No measured review-time savings, model precision/recall, live integrations, or audit acceptance are claimed.
