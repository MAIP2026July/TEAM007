# Product roadmap and proof gates

Milestone Plan

### Milestone 1 — Topic and strategy freeze

Submit:

- problem and target user;
- product vision and JTBD;
- opportunity matrix;
- why AI rather than rules alone;
- first wedge and MVP boundary;
- initial build/buy/API recommendation;
- principal risks and proposed evidence of success.

### Milestone 2 — Artifact review

Submit:

- PRD-lite;
- workflow and wireframes;
- feature prioritization;
- MVP definition;
- roadmap and proof gates;
- data/model assumptions;
- initial benchmark and labeling plan.

### Milestone 3 — Evaluation and risk review

Submit:

- technical, business, trust, and governance metrics;
- baseline comparison;
- economics and scaling note;
- ethics and risk note;
- launch blockers;
- prototype evidence and limitations.

### Final package

1. Executive summary.
2. Final report.
3. Presentation deck.
4. Prototype readme or link.
5. AI collaboration log.
6. Individual contribution statement.
7. Appendix with PRD, wireframes, metrics, roadmap, risk note, and prototype evidence.

---

## Expansion gate
Expansion is conditional on evidence quality, false-clear performance, reviewer trust and data-handling approval.
